Watched countries side by side

Each country's currency and local food readings next to each other, per US dollar (a rise is a weaker currency). Daily rates are the ECB's; official monthly averages are the World Bank's Global Economic Monitor; market rates are those WFP collects in local markets, many of them unofficial. "Market against official" compares the median market rate with the official average for the same month. Nothing here is combined into a score.

Local food prices in watched countries

Median three-month change across each country's market price series. Nominal prices in local currency, from WFP. Data arrives one to three months late.

Sanctions lists

What changed between Headwind's own readings of six official lists: the US Treasury's SDN list, the UN Security Council's consolidated list, the EU's financial sanctions list, the UK Sanctions List, France's asset-freeze register and Canada's consolidated autonomous sanctions list. Most of France's entries restate EU and UN measures; its own national freezes are counted and scored apart, and its restatements are counted only. A list read for the first time sets its baseline; changes are counted from then. People and organizations are counts only; their names are on the publisher's list. Vessels are named, with their IMO numbers. An entry that stays on a list and changes its programs or type is counted as changed, and a listed vessel whose name, IMO, MMSI, flag or type changes is named before and after. The date is when Headwind saw the change, not the date of the designation.

Export-control, sanctions and trade rules

Rules and proposed rules published in the US Federal Register by BIS, OFAC and USTR, USTR's notices (Section 301 actions and exclusions), BIS's and ITA's Section 232 notices, and presidential documents that are tariff actions, in the last 60 days, as they were titled.

Tariff actions and the tariff schedule

How many presidential tariff actions the Federal Register published, and how many revisions of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule the US International Trade Commission posted, in the last 28 days, each against the 27 windows of 28 days before. Unusual means a robust z of 3.5 or more and at least four documents or three revisions. Actions the White House has signed that the Federal Register does not yet carry are listed apart.

Filed for public inspection

Filings by BIS, OFAC and USTR at the Office of the Federal Register, open to the public a business day or more before they are published, kept for 30 days. HW-IND-2 is met when a BIS or USTR stay, suspension, extension or Entity List filing comes within 14 days before a date that an earlier document of the same rule, docket or investigation set, or a date the filing itself says was scheduled. It is a measurement, not a judgment of why.

US export-control and screening lists

The Entity List and the other US Commerce, State and Treasury lists, read from trade.gov's Consolidated Screening List. What changed between Headwind's own readings, as counts by the address country each list gives. Counts only; the names are on the publishers' lists. An entry that stays on a list and changes its programs, type or address country is counted as changed. trade.gov assigns the ids of the BIS and State lists itself; an entry that comes back under a new id with the same name and type is counted once, as the same entry.

US import reliance for mineral commodities

Net import reliance is imports less exports, adjusted for stock changes, as a share of US consumption. Figures are annual, from the US Geological Survey, and are copied as published.

Sources and their state

A source that failed shows its last good copy and says so. Fresh, late and stale compare a source's newest data with its own rhythm (business days for the ECB, months for FAO and WFP); read means it was read and its data carry no date to judge. A note flags a reading much smaller than usual, or a publisher date that has not moved for a long time.
