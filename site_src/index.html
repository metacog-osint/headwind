Economic stress, measured against its own normal.

Each currency, energy price and food price is compared with its own past changes. An item is marked unusual only when the move is far outside that range. These are measurements, not forecasts and not investment advice.

Outside the normal range

Robust z of 3.5 or more against the series' own history, and a move above the lane's floor.

Currencies against the US dollar

Five business days. ECB reference rates, which cover about 30 currencies and not the rouble.

Energy

Five trading days. EIA spot prices, a day or two behind.

FAO food price indices

Change on the month; 2014–2016 = 100. FAO revises earlier months.

Local food prices in watched countries

Median three-month change across each country's market price series. Nominal prices in local currency, from WFP. Data arrives one to three months late.

Sanctions lists

What changed between Headwind's own readings of each official list. People and organizations are counts only; their names are on the publisher's list. Vessels are named, with their IMO numbers. The date is when Headwind saw the change, not the date of the designation.

Export-control and sanctions rules

Rules and proposed rules published in the US Federal Register by BIS and OFAC in the last 60 days, as the agencies titled them.

US export-control and screening lists

The Entity List and the other US Commerce, State and Treasury lists, read from trade.gov's Consolidated Screening List. What changed between Headwind's own readings, as counts by the address country each list gives. Counts only; the names are on the publishers' lists. An entry a publisher edits can count as one removed and one added.

US import reliance for mineral commodities

Net import reliance is imports less exports, adjusted for stock changes, as a share of US consumption. Figures are annual, from the US Geological Survey, and are copied as published.

Coming dates

From the publishers' own schedules. US inflation and jobs dates are not covered yet.
